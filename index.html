    How does this work?

    Enter the lectures held so far and how many you attended. Then choose how many upcoming lectures you'll attend or skip. Projected attendance = (attended + upcoming attended) ÷ (conducted + upcoming attended + upcoming skipped) × 100. Insights use the same rule: the skip limit is the most lectures you can miss in a row while staying at or above your target, and the attend count is the fewest you must attend in a row to reach it.
